## 1. What we collect
- Your Telegram account details we receive from Telegram: user ID, name, username, language.
- What you send: messages, photos, files, voice notes, image descriptions, and the replies you get. Chats you save are kept until you delete them.
- Your settings: memory on/off, saved memories, your Extended models choice and when you made it.
- Usage counts (messages, tokens, images, files, voice seconds) to apply your plan's limits, and your plan and payment status (payments are handled by Telegram Stars; we never see your card).

## 2. Why we use it
To answer you, keep your chat history, apply limits, prevent abuse, and keep the service working. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads based on your chats.

## 3. Who processes your messages (AI services)
Your messages, and anything you attach, are sent to AI services run by other companies in order to produce a reply. Those services process
the data outside Ethiopia.

- **Private models** (used for everyone): their providers state that they do not use API data to train their models. They may keep requests for a short time to check for abuse.
- **Extended models** (only if you turn it on): extra models that run on free services from other companies. Those companies may keep your messages and the replies, use them to improve their own AI, and in some cases let their staff read them. We cannot control that.
- If you have not answered the Extended models question, or you chose to keep your chats private, your messages only go to the private models.
- Web search sends only the words it searches for to search services, whether Extended models is on or off.

## 4. Sensitive information
Please do not send passwords, ID or bank details, health records, or other people's private details, whether Extended models is on or off.

## 5. Your choices
- Change Extended models any time in Settings (or `/privacy` in the chat). It applies to messages you send afterwards; it cannot take back what was already sent.
- Delete chats in the app (this also deletes the files attached to them). Delete saved memories in Settings.
- Ask us to see, correct or delete your data, or to withdraw your consent: [contact]. We answer within [30] days.
